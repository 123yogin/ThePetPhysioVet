## 8. Off-site actions that decide "pet vet ahmedabad"-type rankings

1. **Claim and verify the Google Business Profile** at the Shilaj address. Check whether it exists already, and if it is a service-area business, set service areas honestly.
2. **Categories:** primary should describe the true service (e.g. a veterinary rehabilitation / animal physiotherapy category if available in GBP). Adding "Veterinarian" or "Veterinary care" only if the clinic legitimately operates as one. Mislabeling risks suspension.
3. **Services list and description** with real services: physiotherapy, home visits, IVDD, post-surgical rehab, and so on. Add Products/services with descriptions, hours, phone, and the website URL pointing to `/`.
4. **Reviews:** ask satisfied clients for reviews that name the condition and the area ("IVDD rehab", "Shilaj"). Reply to every review. Never buy or gate reviews.
5. **Photos and posts:** real clinic, session and home-visit photos (with owner consent). A weekly post is an easy cadence.
6. **Citations (NAP consistent):** Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART where relevant, Drlogy, Practo-style listings, Yappe, pet directories, Facebook and Instagram. Same name, address, phone everywhere.
7. **Local referral and links:** referral relationships with local vets who refer rehab cases (these link and cite naturally), Ahmedabad pet communities, animal welfare groups (e.g. PFA Ahmedabad), and local news or blogs.
8. **Search Console and Bing Webmaster:** verify, submit the sitemap, and monitor queries. Annotate change dates.
9. **Schema:** `LocalBusiness`/`VeterinaryCare` with sameAs links to GBP and socials. Ensure it matches GBP and visible content.
